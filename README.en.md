# Hi, I'm Jeisson Rocha da Cunha

### Systems Analyst | Full Stack Developer | Databases & SQL

IT professional with more than **18 years of experience** in systems analysis, software development, and the evolution of web and business solutions. I enjoy understanding problems, organizing requirements, and turning business needs into useful solutions.

I am co-founder and director of **Air Tecnologia**. My professional background combines software development, requirements gathering, databases, project management, and client relationships. Over the years, I have worked on both custom-built systems and the evolution of solutions used in real-world contexts.

My established experience includes PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, C#, and SQL Server. In more recent projects, I have been working with React, TypeScript, Node.js, REST APIs, and Flutter, while also exploring automation and applications of Artificial Intelligence.
