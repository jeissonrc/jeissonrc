# Hi, I'm Jeisson Rocha da Cunha

### Systems Analyst | Full Stack Developer | Database Specialist

IT professional with more than **18 years of experience** in systems analysis and web solution development, ranging from websites to business applications.

I am co-founder and director of **Air Tecnologia**, where I have built a career combining software development, requirements analysis, databases, project management, client relationships, and the development of customized solutions.

My main experience has been built around **PHP and MySQL**. I am currently expanding my stack with **React, Node.js, Tailwind CSS, and Flutter**, while also exploring **Artificial Intelligence and automation**, maintaining a focus on building complete solutions, from Front-end to Back-end.
